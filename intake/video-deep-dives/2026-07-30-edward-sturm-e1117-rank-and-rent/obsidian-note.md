# Rank & Rent SEO — Ippei Kanehara (The Edward Show E1117)

**One-liner**: Rank your own site under your own invented local brand for BOF
"{service} {city}" searches, put a tracking number with a **whisper message** on it, and
rent the ringing phone: free leads → commission → $1–2K/month flat rent, billed weekly.
A ~15-hour tree-care site has paid ~$2K/month for 11 years ($240K+). 130 new sites built
last year to re-test the playbook.

## Why it matters to us
- Same BOF commercial-intent doctrine as [[AFFILIATE-ENGINE]] — a ranked local site is a
  permanently-owned bridge page with a phone number; whisper + free-leads close maps to
  pay-per-call offers.
- His content blog (ippei.com) lost ~90% of clicks to core updates while 11-year-old
  rank-and-rent sites never moved → weight portfolios toward commercial intent.
- Niche filter + Maps-pack demand proxy drop straight into [[market-engine]] packs.

## The formula (condensed, ✅ = verbatim-grounded)
1. ✅ Niche: phone-driven home construction, avg job ≥$2–3K, fast close. Kill: carpet
   cleaning (commoditized), plumbing/HVAC (saturated), remodels/ADU (slow close). Hunt
   sub-niches (epoxy floors, garage storage).
2. ✅ City: 80K min, **100–300K sweet spot**. Demand proxy = 10–15 businesses in the Maps
   pack; ignore keyword-tool volume (local BOF often reads zero and still pays).
3. ✅ Vet the SERP by hand: top 3–5 sites — DR ≤5, ~10 indexed pages, broken headings,
   accidental targeting → enter. DR 30+ across the top (PI law) → walk.
4. ✅ Brand: partial-EMD "{City} Quality {Service}"; true EMDs are taken.
5. ✅ Hub page: brand-first H1 + title; first 2 paragraphs define the entity (brand +
   years + exact services); entity-load with materials/engineering vocabulary; write AS
   the brand — tenant ends up running two brands, "a franchise, but better."
6. ✅ Coverage: 30–40 targeted BOF pages beats a 10-page incumbent **without backlinks**.
7. ✅ Phone: tracking number + whisper ("this lead was sent by …"); Stripe card-on-file,
   **weekly billing** ($250/wk < $1K/mo psychologically).
8. ⏳ First-30-days launch sequence (51:09).
9. ⏳ Keyword mining: autocomplete + GSC; new-page-vs-expand rule (43:34–48:31).
10. ✅ GBP = optional upside (video verification, ~30–40% of his sites have one; student
    does $15–20K/mo with none); urgency niches (towing) GBP-driven, consideration niches
    (cabinets) website-driven. ⏳ verification + review detail (57:59–1:05:52).
11. ✅ Monetize: free leads → commission → flat rent; whale tier = call-tracking
    API-wired to tenant CRM, straight commission (his LA construction deal: 7 niches,
    dozens of sites). ⏳ contracts/leverage (1:16:16), handoff scripts (1:10:08).
12. ✅ Scale tenant first: service businesses travel 20–30 mi → sibling-city sites take a
    client from $500 to $2–3K/mo. ⏳ mega brand rollup (1:21:51).

## Secrets worth remembering
- **The whisper message is the sales engine** — tenant hears your name before every job.
- **Sell with a ringing phone, not a pitch** — free leads make the close trivial to a
  contractor pre-burned by $500/mo junk agencies.
- **Bill weekly** — churn hack; tenants unspoken-to for years keep paying.
- **Saturation is SERP-level** — most local incumbents rank *accidentally*; targeting on
  purpose beats them.
- **Topical authority > backlinks** at local scale (30–40 pages vs 10).
- **"Bottom of the funnel doesn't die"** — update-resilience thesis, proven in his own
  portfolio both ways.
