# Rank & Rent SEO — Ippei Kanehara (The Edward Show E1117)

**One-liner**: Rank a small site for bottom-of-funnel "{service} {city}" searches, then rent
the asset to a local operator — per-lead → commission → flat monthly. A ~15-hour build has
paid Ippei $240K+ over a decade. 130 new sites built recently to test what still works.

## Why it matters to us
- Same BOF commercial-intent doctrine as [[AFFILIATE-ENGINE]] — a ranked local site is a
  permanently-owned bridge page with a phone number.
- Update-resilient vs content sites → portfolio weighting argument.
- Niche filter reusable in [[market-engine]] packs: phone-driven, high-ticket, low-loyalty.

## The formula (condensed)
1. Niche: phone-driven home service, high ticket, low customer loyalty, urgent.
2. City: ~75K–250K population, not mega-metro; validate demand.
3. Vet competitors page-level — many local sites are still thin.
4. Domain: EMD/PMD or local-brandable (must absorb neighbor cities later).
5. Structure: homepage authority hub → service pages → sub-city pages; strict heading
   hierarchy; internal links hub→spokes; no doorway footprints (unique local proof per page).
6. Content: AI draft → entity optimization → human edit → local uniqueness.
7. Keywords: Autocomplete + GSC mining for overlooked variants; defined new-page-vs-expand rule.
8. Links: skip guest posts; topical coverage + internal authority across owned network.
9. Monetize: free leads to a picked operator → commission → flat monthly rent; keep
   domain/site/tracking-number ownership in the contract.
10. Scale: neighbor cities, multiple sites per tenant, eventual brand rollup.

## Secrets worth remembering
- **Leads before clients** — sell with a ringing phone, not a pitch.
- **GBP is optional** — biggest misconception; organic + call tracking is enough to bill.
- **Flat rent churns less than commission** — fixed bill on a working phone line stays paid.
- **Saturation is angle-level** — judged per-SERP, not per-niche.
- **Topical authority replaced backlinks** in Ippei's current playbook.
